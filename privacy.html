Privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

We keep things simple. This policy explains how Isotalo Consulting Oy handles personal data when you visit isotalo.fi or contact us.

Who we are

Isotalo Consulting Oy, Helsinki, Finland, is responsible for your personal data (the “controller”). For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@isotalo.fi.

What we collect and why

When you visit, our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical details such as your IP address, browser information, pages requested, and request times. This helps deliver and protect the site. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable website.

When you contact us, we use the details you provide, such as your name, email address, and message, to respond and discuss working together. This is based on our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries or on taking steps towards a contract at your request. Sharing these details is voluntary, but we need a way to reply.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or visitor analytics. The site itself does not set cookies or store data in your browser. Our hosting provider may use essential cookies for security. If we introduce optional cookies that require consent, we will ask first.

Mobile apps and connected accounts

We develop iOS and Android apps that may connect with Instagram, Facebook, and, where available, TikTok. These connections are optional: you choose whether to connect an account and grant permissions through the platform’s authorization process. Apps use the information you authorize to provide the connected features.

Each app’s privacy notice explains its integrations, the data it accesses, how it uses and keeps that data, and how to disconnect or request deletion. You can also manage granted permissions through the relevant platform’s account settings. Those platforms process data under their own privacy policies.

Analytics in our apps

Apps we develop may use PostHog’s EU-hosted analytics to understand how features are used and improve the experience. Depending on the app, this may include usage events and device information. Each app’s privacy notice explains what is collected, how long it is kept, and your choices. Where consent is required, we ask before enabling analytics. Read more about PostHog’s privacy practices.

Providers and data sharing

We use service providers for website hosting, security, and email. We may also share information when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

Cloudflare may process technical data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Its transfer safeguards include the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, where applicable. Read more in Cloudflare’s privacy policy and data processing terms, or contact us for details about safeguards.

How long we keep data

We keep messages for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting work, meet legal recordkeeping requirements, or resolve claims. We delete information when it is no longer needed for these purposes. Our hosting provider keeps technical data according to its service and security needs, as described in its privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or receive a portable copy of data processed automatically under a contract or consent.

Email privacy@isotalo.fi to make a request. We normally respond within one month and may need to confirm your identity. We do not use your data for automated decisions or profiling.

You can also complain to Finland’s Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman or your local data protection authority.

Links and updates

Other websites we link to have their own privacy policies. We may update this policy as our services change; the date above shows the latest revision.
